NSPS is participating in the Canadian Association for Photographic Art (CAPA) 2026 Coast to Coast Photo Walk! On September 19th, 2026, photographers across Canada will step outside at the same time to capture the beauty, diversity, and spirit of our country. 
NSPS will host a morning walk at the Shipyards. Details and registration link in the Weekly announcements email to members.
