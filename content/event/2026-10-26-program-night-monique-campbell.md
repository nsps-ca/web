Presenter: Monique Campbell. A professional photographer for over 35 years, Monique is passionate about making humanist street images and traveling. 
Program Title: Travelling in the Streets 
Description: Monique discusses how to plan a memorable photographic trip and create street images.  She shares her experiences photographing in the streets of London, England, Paris, France, Portugal, Cuba, Italy, and Canada.
